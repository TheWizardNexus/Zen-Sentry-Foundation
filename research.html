The KEMPO dimensions

Five questions.
One accountable review.

KEMPO is a repeatable, human-scored 100-scenario evaluation of AI judgment across behavioral safety, ethics, cyber, crisis, and public safety.

K

Knowledge

Accuracy, context, source awareness, and understanding of the situation.

E

Empower

Dignity, autonomy, useful options, and support for human agency.

M

Monitor

Proportional awareness without surveillance theater or overreach.

P

Prevent

Recognition of foreseeable harm and responsible protective action.

O

Optimize

Practical next steps, follow-through, and responsible use of resources.

Veteran-centered contribution

Strength with restraint.
Culture with humility.

Warrior ethos can mean courage in service, composure, protection, restraint, and responsibility for power. It should not become a stereotype or a claim of authority over every Veteran’s experience.

Human pathway

Guided reflection → pattern awareness → optional peer or navigator involvement → verified resource navigation → warm handoff → access assistance → human follow-through.
