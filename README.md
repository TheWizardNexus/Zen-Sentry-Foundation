## The mission

Zen Sentry Foundation is a veteran-owned and operated Texas nonprofit organization recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. It advances upstream behavioral health, Veteran suicide prevention, responsible AI ethics, and human-centered systems that strengthen support before crisis. Its public work sits within The Wizard Nexus ecosystem while the organizations keep their legal and governance boundaries explicit.

The operating path is deliberately human:

> **Earlier insight → Trusted connection → Practical follow-through**

Zen represents composure. Sentry represents early awareness and protection. Foundation represents durable support. Together, they frame technology as a way to help people notice, connect, and follow through—not as a replacement for care, judgment, or human responsibility.

## Current public work

- **Zen Sentry Veteran Resiliency & Early Support Program** — a grant-supported program in development, selected for a 2026 **$6,000 CenterPoint Energy Foundation Energizing Veteran Resilience Grant**.
- **Connected veteran pathway** — structured reflection through PreCrisis, reviewed resource navigation through the ZSF Veteran Resource Library built on BOSS Library Core, veteran-centered Warrior Spirit work, and trusted human support.
- **KEMPO human-safety research** — Zen Sentry helps bring a veteran-centered perspective to The Wizard Nexus evaluation work. Six current model runs produced 600 answer-complete responses; accountable human scoring remains the next gate.

The site labels planned work as planned and does not report outcomes that have not been observed.

## Source boundaries

This repository contains the public, dependency-free website. It does not contain private research responses, evaluator identities, Veteran records, health information, or controlled program artifacts. A public site does not imply that every underlying system or dataset is open source.

KEMPO is an evaluation framework—not therapy, diagnosis, emergency response, or a substitute for qualified care or human judgment.

## Crisis support

Veterans and their loved ones can **Dial 988 then Press 1**, [chat online](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/get-help-now/chat/), or text **838255** for free, confidential, 24/7 support. Zen Sentry Foundation is not a crisis service.
