Who can strengthen the pathway

Bring your context.
Keep the work grounded.

Partnership is most useful when the people closest to a community help define the need, the boundary, the evidence, and what responsible follow-through requires.

  • Veteran-serving organizationsLocal needs, peer pathways, trust, outreach, and responsible program delivery.
  • Peer leaders and navigatorsShared experience, warm handoffs, reviewed resources, and sustained connection.
  • Behavioral-health professionalsCare boundaries, referral context, resilience education, and qualified judgment.
  • Resource providersMental health, benefits, housing, transportation, basic needs, and access support.
  • Researchers and evaluatorsMethods, scoring, cultural humility, privacy, and honest program learning.
  • Responsible technologistsHuman-centered tools, public-interest infrastructure, accessibility, security, and reviewability.

Start with signal

A useful first message
answers five questions.

Clear context lets the right people respond without asking a community to repeat its story or exposing sensitive details in a public channel.

01

Who is affected?

Name the community or role without sharing private records or health information.

02

What is happening?

Describe the need, current evidence, source, and what has already been tried.

03

What decision is next?

State the responsible owner, the boundary, and the smallest useful next step.

Immediate support

This site is not
a crisis service.

If you are a Veteran in crisis or concerned about one, connect with the Veterans Crisis Line now. You do not need to be enrolled in VA benefits or health care.

Call

Dial 988 then Press 1 for free, confidential support from a qualified responder, 24 hours a day.

Chat or text

Chat through VeteransCrisisLine.net or text 838255. Support is available for Veterans, service members, families, friends, and people concerned about someone.
